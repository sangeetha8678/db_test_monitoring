# BugBags Equipment Maintenance & Alarm Codes Guide

## Alarm Codes & Diagnostic Procedures
* **Alarm Code 10 (Low Power Factor Warning):** Triggered when Power Factor drops below 0.85 for more than 15 minutes. Procedure: Inspect power factor correction capacitors and automatic switching relays.
* **Alarm Code 23 (Phase Overvoltage / Voltage Imbalance Trip):** Triggered when line voltage imbalance exceeds 5.0% or voltage spike exceeds 460V. Procedure: Immediately switch equipment to standby, measure phase-to-neutral voltages ($V_{AN}, V_{BN}, V_{CN}$), and verify transformer tap settings.
* **Alarm Code 45 (Current Overload Surge):** Triggered when phase current exceeds 150% of rated capacity. Procedure: Inspect motor bearings and mechanical load binding.

## Routine Maintenance Schedule
* **Quarterly:** Thermographic camera inspection of busbars and terminal connections.
* **Annually:** Calibration of digital telemetry energy meters and current transformers (CTs).
