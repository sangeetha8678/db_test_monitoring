# BugBags Enterprise Power Quality & Meter Manual

## 1. Power Factor Guidance
* **Nominal Power Factor:** 0.92 – 1.00
* **Acceptable Threshold:** >= 0.85
* **Low Efficiency Warning:** < 0.85 (incurs reactive power penalties and increased line losses).
* **Remediation:** Engage automatic capacitor banks or active harmonic filters.

## 2. Voltage Imbalance Standards (NEMA MG-1)
* **Maximum Acceptable Imbalance:** 2.0%
* **Warning Limit:** 2.0% to 5.0% (requires load rebalancing across phases AB, BC, CA).
* **Critical Emergency Limit:** > 5.0% (causes severe induction motor overheating and insulation degradation).

## 3. Current Phase Imbalance Standards
* **Nominal Imbalance:** < 5.0%
* **Attention Threshold:** 10.0%
* **Critical Alarm Threshold:** > 20.0%

## 4. Grid Frequency Specifications
* **Nominal Frequency:** 50.0 Hz
* **Operating Range:** 49.5 Hz to 50.5 Hz
